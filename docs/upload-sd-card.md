# Upload an SD card

Photos from an SD card go through the same analysis as the photos a camera sends over 4G. They never trigger species alerts, and they don't slow down the live cameras. Project admins find it under Admin tools, Bulk upload.

## Upload a folder

1. Click Bulk upload.
2. Drop the folder from the SD card in the window, or click to pick it. One folder holds the photos of one camera.
3. Check the numbers and the sample photos. A camera the app knows is found from its photos. For any other camera, pick the site where the photos were taken.
4. Click Upload and keep the tab open until the upload is done. You stay logged in while it runs.

Then the analysis runs on the server and you can close the tab. When the job is done, click "Review in curation" to check the results.

## Already in the project

A photo is already in the project when the same file is there, or when the camera already sent it over 4G. A 4G camera sends a smaller copy under another name. These photos are left out, so the 4G photo and its alerts stay as they are.

So you can upload a card again without making doubles. Only the new photos go up.

## Why photos are skipped

Before the upload, the Review upload step warns about photos without a date or that can't be read. Those don't go up.

After the analysis, the job says how many photos were skipped and why. Click Log for the list per photo.

- Skipped without location. The camera needs GPS on every photo, and this one had none. Often the first photos after placing a camera, before it found its position. Nothing to do.
- Skipped from an unknown camera. The photo comes from another camera model than the rest of the folder. Upload each camera on its own.
- Skipped as unreadable. The file is damaged. Upload it again, and if it still fails, the file is broken.

## When an upload stops

When files don't arrive, for example because the connection drops, the upload pauses. The job says "Paused. Resume to send the rest of the folder."

Click Resume and pick the same folder again. Only the missing files go up.

A paused upload waits 7 days, then it is cancelled. Stop ends a job at any time. Photos that were already analysed stay in the project.

## Limits

- JPEG photos only, up to 50 MB each.
- Up to 20,000 photos per upload.
- One camera per folder.
